# From the quiet room into the questions

Two modular transparent PNG assets, created with the built-in image generation tool. Source artwork remains unchanged.

- `seeker-kneeling.png`: a new curious/kneeling pose of the existing traveller. Also used briefly when landing.
- `curled-research-page.png`: a separate paper leaf, animated at three depths during the fall.

The existing `research-notebook.png`, `seeker-falling.png` and `seeker-standing.png` are reused. Book leaves separate through browser transforms; no complete scene is baked into a new image.

## Scene integration and review

The quiet room uses amber light, copper wood and burgundy shadows to match the cottage window. The book and kneeling traveller sit on the foreground floor, with an adjusted baseline for short phones.

Research sequence: reading and unfolding → loss of support → camera following the fall through loose pages → kneeling landing → standing pause → archive reading and notebook close-up. Every pose and paper position derives from scroll, including reverse scrolling. The original 04–05 doorway effect is retained.

The header action first focuses the question in the book; a subsequent shortcut reaches the archive. The archive's own continue action remains inactive until arrival. Reduced-motion mode stacks the environments and hides the falling sequence.

Checked locally at desktop and mobile sizes, including 430 × 667. Preview captures covered the warm room, unfolding, fall, landing, archive wide shot and close-up. The scroll/navigation checks in `scripts/verify.cjs` pass at heights 900, 844 and 667.
