- `research-book-depth.png`: connected spine and nested curved paper layers, replacing split halves and the flat oval. The existing research notebook remains the resting state.
- `art-journal-closed.png`: closed cover for an explicit opening interaction.
- `art-journal-open.png`: blank, unobstructed pages; the original drawings are separate interactive elements placed on top. Full images open in a native dialog with Close, Escape, and a direct-image fallback.

Scroll timing and falling trajectory are unchanged in this revision.
